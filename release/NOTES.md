# Wing to wing

## Better

- **Mini player:** the speaker's name and the line under it are a size larger and read more easily at a glance; the cover and the buttons stay where they were.
- **Speakers on Home:** the speaker you are using has its card in the row too, so the row tells at a glance what plays where, and a card no longer leaves the row once you tap it.
- **Earlier versions:** the five newest versions stay on the releases page, so going back a step stays easy.

## Fixed

- **Grouped speakers:** with every speaker grouped, Home's Speakers row was empty, though all of them were playing through one. It now shows that group and what it plays.
