# Crumb hunt

## New

- **Speakers on Home:** every card has its own play button, as the mini player does. A dark pause means that speaker is playing; tap it to pause there, or tap play to carry on, without switching speakers. The cards are wider, so names fit and the next one peeks in.
- **A better first Home:** new installs start with Search, the speakers, Continue listening, your pins, and rows of recent music, playlists, new albums and favourite stations.

## Better

- **Mini player:** the play button is dark, so it catches the eye first.
- **Follow the music:** the speaker choices look as they did before.
- **German:** clearer wording in several places, such as „Lautsprecher“ above „Dieses Handy“.
