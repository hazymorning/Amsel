# Dusk song

## New

- **Open source:** Amsel's code is now public, under the GPL. This version is signed anew: remove the old Amsel once, install this one, and sign in again.

## Better

- **One logic on Home:** everything that plays at a tap asks first, Continue listening included; only what is playing right now opens Now Playing straight away.
- **Speaker button:** the mini player's speaker button wears the grey of the tabs below it, its glyph in the same ink as play.
- **Play on:** Group and Ungroup have as much room as the speaker button in Now Playing.
- **Settings:** at night the blackbird stands before a quiet moon, and the line beneath simply reads © 2026 hazymorning.

## Fixed

- **Mini player:** a title too long for its room glides to its end and back again, as it used to.
