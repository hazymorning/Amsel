# Window perch

## New

- **Settings, easy to find:** a small gear now sits at the end of the search field at the top of Home. Tap it to open Settings; tap anywhere else in the field to search, as before. It works just like the account picture in the search bar of many Google apps, so it should feel familiar. Pulling Home down still opens Settings too, if you are used to that.

## Better

- **The tip about Settings** now points to the gear first, and only then to pulling Home down.
- **In Edit Home** the search field stays a plain section that you can move, without the gear, so editing your Home never opens Settings by mistake.
